An intermediate undergraduate mathematics course bridging pre-university algebra with calculus and discrete mathematics topics relevant to computing and applied sciences.

**Role:** Visiting Lecturer

**Offered:** Fall 2020, Spring 2021

---

#### Topics Covered

- Sets, logic, and proof techniques
- Functions, relations, and graphs
- Matrices and determinants
- Complex numbers
- Sequences, series, and mathematical induction
- Differentiation and integration techniques
- Applications of calculus
