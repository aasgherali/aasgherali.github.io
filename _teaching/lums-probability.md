Served as Teaching Assistant for this foundational course in probability theory, supporting two consecutive summer cohorts. Conducted problem-solving sessions and assisted students with both theoretical proofs and applied problem sets.

**Role:** Teaching Assistant

**Offered:** Summer 2021–22, Summer 2022–23

---

#### Topics Covered

- Sample spaces, events, and axioms of probability
- Conditional probability and independence
- Discrete random variables and distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Geometric)
- Continuous random variables and distributions (Uniform, Normal, Exponential)
- Joint distributions and marginal densities
- Expectation, variance, and covariance
- Law of Large Numbers and Central Limit Theorem
- Introduction to Markov chains
