A course covering line integrals (scalar and vector), surface integrals, and the theorems of Green, Gauss, and Stokes. In the second part of the course, we will cover the theory of ODEs limited to first and second order linear ODEs. 

**Role:** Asstistant Professor

**Offered:** Fall 2025–26

**Topics covered:**
- Line integrals (scalar and vector)
- Surface integrals
- Green's theorem
- Stokes' theorem
- Gauss' theorem
- First order linear ODEs (Separable, Exact, Integrating Factor, Bernoulli)
- Second order linear ODEs (Homogeneous, Non-homogeneous, Method of Undetermined Coefficients, Variation of Parameters)
