An introductory course in single-variable calculus covering limits, continuity, differentiation, integration, and sequences and series. Emphasis is placed on both conceptual understanding and computational proficiency, with applications drawn from the natural and social sciences.

**Role:** Asstistant Professor

**Offered:** Fall 2024–25

**Topics covered:**
- Limits and continuity
- Differentiation techniques
- Applications of derivatives
- Integration and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- Sequences and series
