A course covering advanced techniques for solving complicated ordinary and partial differential equations, alongside foundational numerical methods. Topics include modeling physical phenomena, power series solutions, Fourier expansions, and analytical algorithmic implementations.
**Role:** Asstistant Professor

**Offered:** Spring 2025–26

**Topics covered:**
- Modeling physical systems with first-order ODEs
- Second-order ODEs with variable coefficients
- Power series solutions and the Frobenius method
- Fourier Series and orthogonal functions
- Solutions to boundary value problems and Sturm-Liouville methods
- Subtleties of solving Partial Differential Equations (PDEs)
- Separation of Variables method for PDEs
- Numerical solution of ODEs and PDEs in Julia/Python
