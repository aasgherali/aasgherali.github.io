A preparatory course in mathematics designed to bridge foundational algebra and trigonometry with the rigour of university-level calculus. Students develop fluency in functions, graphing, and algebraic reasoning necessary for MATH-101.

**Role:** Adjunct Faculty

**Offered:** Spring 2022–23

---

#### Topics Covered

- Functions and their graphs
- Polynomial, rational, exponential, and logarithmic functions
- Trigonometry and the unit circle
- Inverse functions
- Systems of equations and inequalities
- Introduction to sequences and series
