Served as Teaching Assistant for this graduate-level course in numerical methods and analysis. The course develops rigorous mathematical foundations for numerical algorithms with a focus on accuracy, stability, and convergence.

**Role:** Teaching Assistant

**Offered:** Spring 2019–20

---

#### Topics Covered

- Floating point arithmetic and error analysis
- Root-finding methods (bisection, Newton-Raphson, secant)
- Numerical linear algebra (LU, QR, iterative solvers)
- Interpolation and polynomial approximation
- Numerical differentiation and integration (quadrature)
- Numerical solutions to ordinary differential equations
- Finite difference methods for PDEs
