A course in quantitative methods for undergraduate students in business and management. Emphasis on developing mathematical and statistical tools to support data-driven decision making.

**Role:** Visiting Lecturer

**Offered:** Fall 2020

---

#### Topics Covered

- Mathematical functions for business applications
- Linear programming and graphical methods
- Matrix algebra for economic models
- Differentiation for optimisation
- Descriptive and inferential statistics
- Time series and forecasting basics
- Decision theory
