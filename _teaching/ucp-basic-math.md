A foundational undergraduate mathematics course covering essential algebra, functions, and introductory calculus for students in business, computing, and social science programmes.

**Role:** Visiting Lecturer

**Offered:** Fall 2019, Fall 2020

---

#### Topics Covered

- Real numbers and algebraic expressions
- Linear and quadratic equations and inequalities
- Functions: definition, domain, range, graphing
- Exponential and logarithmic functions
- Introduction to limits and derivatives
- Basic integration
