A course extending fundamental calculus techniques to multivariable functions and vectors. Topics include parametric graphing, analytical operations on vector products, optimization across boundaries, polar integrations, and vector field line pathways.

**Role:** Asstistant Professor

**Offered:** Spring 2024–25

**Topics covered:**
- Volumes of Revolution
- Parametric Equations and Graphs
- Polar Coordinates and Polar Functions
- Functions of Several Variables
- Partial Derivatives
- Vectors: Dot and Cross Products
- Directional Derivatives
- Optimization: Lagrange Multipliers
- Integrating Functions of Several Variables
- Multiple Integrals
- Parametrization of Curves in 3-Space
- Line Integrals
