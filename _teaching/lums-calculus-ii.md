#### Topics Covered

- Sequences and series; convergence tests
- Power series and Taylor/Maclaurin expansions
- Vectors in 2D and 3D; dot and cross products
- Multivariable functions, partial derivatives, gradient
- Optimization of functions of several variables
- Double and triple integrals
- Introduction to vector calculus (Green's theorem, Stokes' theorem)
