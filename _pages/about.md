Welcome to my webpage! :wave: A dedicated Assistant Professor at the Integrated Sciences and Mathematics Program within the Dhanani School of Science and Engineering, nested within the vibrant academic landscape of Habib University, Karachi. A dedicated teacher committed to imparting knowledge in a liberal arts institution in Pakistan. My intellectual pursuits revolve around the realm of optimal control, specifically as it pertains to continuous models stemming from antibiotic and radiobiological dosing challenges.

With a profound curiosity for the practical applications of mathematics, I am driven to bridge the gap between theory and real-world impact. The complex landscapes of antibiotic and radiobiological dosing have captivated my attention, motivating me to explore optimal control methodologies that hold the potential to revolutionize healthcare strategies and deepen our understanding of biological systems.

My journey is not confined to the theoretical alone. My explorations encompass a diverse array of interests, ranging from computational mathematics and numerical optimization to the intricacies of neural network inspired PDEs. I find great fulfillment in engaging with modeling and simulations, as well as diving into the depths of data driven parameter estimation and model identification. 

<p><strong>Research interests</strong>: computational mathematics, numerical optimization, machine learning for public health, modeling and simulations, optimal control theory, parameter estimation, stochastic modeling.
